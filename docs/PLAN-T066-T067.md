### T-067 — the join CTA is offered unconditionally
✅ **The server is not at risk and needs no change.** `OfferPassengerService:100-120` looks up any
existing row for `(offer_id, passenger_id)` and throws a **translated 400** for every status:
`offers.alreadyJoined` (`pending`/`confirmed` and the fallback), **`offers.cannotJoinAfterRejected`**,
**`offers.cannotJoinAfterCancelled`**.

the passenger picks seats, confirms a price in a dialog, and is then refused.

✅ **T-055 already modelled the two shapes on this type** — the doc block at `api/offers.ts:23,35`

⚠️ **The driver app matched `offer_id` across string AND number forms** (T-042 ③ check). Do not
assume the id types agree.
